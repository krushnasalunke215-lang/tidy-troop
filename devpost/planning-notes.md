# Planning notes (plan-lens interview, in progress)

Saved so the interview can pick up where it stopped. Not the design brief yet.

Deadline: November 9, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern Time.

## The problem (learner's words)
- "In our room clothes are messy, we don't put them in their places, they are just roaming." Books and plates too.
- Why it happens: "because I'm tired or I'm just lazy." The learner already knows where things go.
- What gets them to tidy: "the room looked clean and fresh."
- What fresh means to them: "clean and clear", "pretty and beautiful", "things are on their own place so room looks organized."
- Real test during the break: "I just put one shirt and one pant to hanger and the bed is clean and it felt good." The good moment is seeing the spot clear.

## Who it's for
- The learner and their roommates: "my roommates struggle with this."

## Decided so far
- The Lens puts a number on each item (clothes, plates, books) and lists about 10 items to put in their places.
- Goal: get a tired person to start and keep going, and "make the user a little disciplined."
- The real reward is the room itself: the bed is clear and the shirt is in the cupboard.

- Small reward: on the spot that just got clear, "a small soldier or a cartoon or something giving salute and saying you are disciplined, whatever the character user chooses."

- Character: an original character (no known cartoons, because of the trademark rule). The learner chose to keep a choice of characters in this version. Risk: each character is another model CLAD has to build and animate.

## Ruled out, with reasons
- Erasing the mess to show a clean room: SPECS can add light but can't hide real objects.
- Sending a photo to AI to remove the clutter and showing the cleaned image: it's a flat picture a phone could show, the preview can't use a live camera, and Fengshui Master (another entry) already repaints rooms with AI.
- Riding the bike (safety), volleyball and table tennis timing (the preview can't track a ball, a jump or a swing), and a coach for talking to people (it would analyze someone who hasn't agreed).

## Preview stand-in
- The preview can't see the real room, so the Lens uses a virtual room (bed, cupboard, desk) with virtual clothes, books and plates, picked up with the mouse.

## Still to decide
1. What happens right where an item goes once you put it away (the small reward).
2. What happens when all 10 are done (the big moment for the video).
3. Where the list of 10 lives, so it isn't a flat panel floating in front of the face.
4. How the hands act on each item.
5. When things appear and when they get out of the way.
6. Three to five done criteria that can be checked in the preview.
7. Character name and personality. The learner wants a clean-themed character with a personality ("Mr Clean or Ms Clean"), but Mr. Clean is a trademarked brand, so it needs an original name.

Dropped by the learner: showing discipline over several days. The Lens covers one tidy-up session.
